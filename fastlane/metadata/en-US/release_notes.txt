JustFling is now Stolnk — rebuilt around one idea: turn a folder into an inbox.

• Pick a name and give any folder its own address, like yourname.stolnk.com/clients
• Files are encrypted in the sender's browser and only your iPhone can open them
• Share any file as a download link with its own expiry and download limit
• No sign-in: your identity is a key kept in your iPhone's Secure Enclave
• Pro is now a one-time purchase, not a subscription
• Delete your account at any time from Me
