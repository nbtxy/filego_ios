JustFling is your private space for files in motion. Receive with one link, organize everything into folders, then download, open, or share files whenever you need them.

RECEIVE
Open any folder and tap "Get File Import Address" to create a public upload link with an expiry. Senders need no app or account: they open the link in a browser, choose files, and everything lands in the folder you selected.

You can also take photos or video, import from Photos or Files, or choose "Open in JustFling" from another app. Terminal users can upload through the same address with curl.

ORGANIZE
• Browse as a list or grid, then find files with breadcrumbs, search, and sorting
• Star, rename, move, or duplicate files so everything lands where it belongs
• Preview images, video, audio, PDFs, text, archives, Markdown, and HTML
• Keep deleted items in Trash for 30 days and see storage use by file type

SEND
Download files, open them in another app, or copy a temporary share link that expires after one day. No permanent public door is left behind.

SAFE AND CONTROLLED
Each import address leads only to the folder you choose. Anyone with the address can upload, but cannot see, download, or delete existing files. Reset it when you're done and the old address stops working immediately.

PRIVACY
Sign in with Apple, and that's the only account there is. No ads. No tracking. No analytics SDKs — we didn't ship any. You can delete your account, and everything in it, from inside the app.

JUSTFLING PRO
The free plan includes 200 MB, unlimited import addresses, and 7-day expiry. Pro includes 50 GB, unlimited import addresses, flexible expiry of 1, 7, 30 or 90 days or never, and direct uploads up to 5 GB per file.
