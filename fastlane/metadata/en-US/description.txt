Stolnk turns your iPhone into an inbox. Pick a name and you get your own address, yourname.stolnk.com. Anyone can open it in a browser and send you files — no app to install, no account to create.

RECEIVE
• Every file is encrypted in the sender's browser before it leaves, and only your iPhone holds the key to open it
• Files land straight in your Received folder, inside the app
• If your phone is away, files wait safely and arrive the next time you open Stolnk
• Pause your address, or protect it with a password (membership) — it stops accepting files at once
• Show the address as a QR code for someone standing next to you

AI OUTPUT, ON YOUR PHONE
• When an AI tool writes an HTML report or a Markdown file, send it from your computer to your receive address and open it on your phone
• HTML renders as a page and Markdown is typeset — not shown as raw source
• No zip, no cable, no email to yourself; the file lands in the same Received folder

SEND (MEMBERSHIP)
• Share any file as a download link that works in any browser
• Choose how long it lasts and how many downloads it allows
• Pause, revoke, or delete a link at any time

YOUR FILES, ON YOUR PHONE
• Browse as a list or grid, sort, rename, and move files between folders; long-press to multi-select for batch move, compress, hide, share, or delete
• Compress files and folders into ZIP archives, and extract ZIP, TAR, TAR.GZ, and GZ archives right on your phone
• Hide sensitive files and folders with a password into a private vault excluded from storage usage — type the password in Search to reveal them
• Import from Photos or Files, and save previewed photos and videos directly to Photos
• Shared Clipboard (yourname.stolnk.com/clip) syncs text in real time between your iPhone and any browser
• Preview images, video, audio, PDFs, text, Markdown, and HTML — including AI-generated pages
• Deleted items stay in Trash for 30 days, and you can see what's using storage

PRIVACY
No sign-in, no email, no password. Your identity is a name and a key kept in your iPhone's Secure Enclave. No ads, no tracking, no analytics SDKs. You can delete your account from inside the app at any time.

FREE, WITH OPTIONAL MEMBERSHIP
Stolnk is free to download. Every iPhone gets one receive address, yourname.stolnk.com, feeding a Received folder. An optional membership raises the limits to files up to 20 GB each and 300 GB of relay per month, adds password protection, lets you create download links, and keeps your subdomain permanently (changeable any time); without it, your custom subdomain lasts 7 days; after that the address stops working and the name is released, so you set a new one to keep receiving. Members can change theirs any time.

Membership has two options: $1.99 per year, an auto-renewing App Store subscription you can cancel any time in your Apple ID settings, or $9.99 once for lifetime, a one-time purchase that does not renew. Both are sold only inside the app. Earlier paid-download buyers get one year of membership free.
