Stolnk turns a folder into an inbox. Pick a name, choose a folder, and you get an address like yourname.stolnk.com/clients. Anyone can open it in a browser and send you files — no app to install, no account to create.

RECEIVE
• Every file is encrypted in the sender's browser before it leaves, and only your iPhone holds the key to open it
• Files land straight in the folder you chose, inside the app
• If your phone is away, files wait safely and arrive the next time you open Stolnk
• Pause an address, protect it with a password, change its path, or reset it — the old address stops working at once
• Show the address as a QR code for someone standing next to you

SEND
• Share any file as a download link that works in any browser
• Choose how long it lasts and how many downloads it allows
• Pause, revoke, or delete a link at any time

YOUR FILES, ON YOUR PHONE
• Browse as a list or grid, sort, rename, and move files between folders
• Import from Photos or Files
• Preview images, video, audio, PDFs, text, Markdown, and HTML
• Deleted items stay in Trash for 30 days, and you can see what's using storage

PRIVACY
No sign-in, no email, no password. Your identity is a name and a key kept in your iPhone's Secure Enclave. No ads, no tracking, no analytics SDKs. You can delete your account from inside the app at any time.

STOLNK PRO
Free includes one receiving address, files up to 2 GB each, 3 GB of relay per month, and one download link at a time. Pro is a one-time purchase — no subscription — that unlocks unlimited addresses, files up to 20 GB each, 300 GB of relay per month, more and longer-lasting download links, and password-protected links.
