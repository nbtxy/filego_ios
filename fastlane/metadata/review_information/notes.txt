ENGLISH

1. No sign-in and no demo account are needed. On first launch choose any available name (3–20 lowercase letters, digits or hyphens). The identity is a key pair generated on the device; there is no email or password.
2. Receiving: each device has exactly one address, https://yourname.stolnk.com (no paths). Open the Received folder with the bottom-left button on the Drive home screen, or Me → address. Open that address in Safari on any device and upload a file; it is encrypted in the browser and arrives in the Received folder. Keep the app in the foreground.
3. Sending: tap ⋯ next to a file → Create download link. The https://yourname.stolnk.com/~code link downloads in any browser. Download links are not end-to-end encrypted; the app says so on the form.
4. Pricing: free download with one optional auto-renewing subscription, Stolnk Membership (com.nbtxy.filego.member.yearly, $1.99/year, group "Stolnk Membership"). It is sold only through StoreKit inside the app (Me → Membership); there is no login, license key, redemption field or external purchase link. The website only describes it and links to the App Store. Non-members can receive files but cannot create download links and use free limits (2 GB files, 3 GB/month relay, no passwords). Members can create links, keep their subdomain permanently (changeable any time), and get 20 GB files, 300 GB/month relay and password-protected links. A non-member's custom subdomain lasts 7 days; then the address stops working and the name is released, and the user must set a new subdomain. Users who bought the previous $1.99 paid download get one year of membership free (granted by our server; no code entry). Terms, price, Restore Purchases, Manage Subscription, Terms of Use (Apple standard EULA) and Privacy Policy links are on the membership screen. Stolnk is iPhone-only; the former Mac and Windows apps are discontinued and nothing is sold outside the App Store.
   Sandbox: use TestFlight or Settings → App Store → Sandbox Account. Tap Create download link (or Me → Membership) and subscribe; a sandbox year renews in about 1 hour. After expiry links pause and the subdomain returns to the 7-day rule. Restore Purchases syncs on a new device; without a subscription, Create download link shows the membership screen.
5. Account deletion: Me → Delete account. It deletes the name, address, links and all server-side data for the device immediately.
6. HTML/Markdown preview is a local file preview, not a browser; remote content is blocked by default.
7. No ads, ATT prompt, analytics SDK or third-party tracking. Backend: https://stolnk.com on Cloudflare.
8. Encryption: only Apple CryptoKit standard algorithms (P-256, AES-GCM, HKDF) for end-to-end encryption; export exemption.

中文

1. 无需登录，也无需演示账号。首次启动时任选一个可用名字（3–20 位小写字母、数字或连字符）即可。身份是设备上生成的密钥对，没有邮箱或密码。
2. 接收：每台设备只有一个地址 https://yourname.stolnk.com（没有路径）。点 Drive 首页左下角的按钮进入「收到的文件」，或在「我的」中查看地址。在任意设备的 Safari 中打开该地址上传文件；文件在浏览器中加密并出现在「收到的文件」里。请保持 App 在前台。
3. 发送：点文件右侧的 ⋯ → 创建下载链接。生成的 https://yourname.stolnk.com/~code 可在任意浏览器下载。下载链接不是端到端加密的，表单上已说明。
4. 定价：App 免费下载，另有一个可选的自动续期订阅「Stolnk 会员」（com.nbtxy.filego.member.yearly，$1.99/年，订阅群组「Stolnk 会员」）。仅通过 App 内 StoreKit 销售（我的 → 会员）；没有登录、授权码、兑换入口或外部购买链接。非会员可接收文件，但不能创建下载链接，且适用免费额度（单文件 2 GB、每月 3 GB 中转、无密码）。会员可创建链接、永久保留子域名（可随时修改），并获得 20 GB 单文件、每月 300 GB 中转和密码保护。非会员的自定义子域名有效期 7 天，到期后地址失效、原名字释放，需要重新设置子域名。购买过此前 $1.99 付费下载的用户免费获得一年会员（服务器自动发放，无需输入代码）。订阅条款、价格、恢复购买、管理订阅、使用条款（Apple 标准 EULA）和隐私政策链接都在会员页。Stolnk 只做 iPhone 版；原 Mac、Windows 版已停售，App Store 以外不再销售任何 Stolnk 内容。
   沙盒：使用 TestFlight，或在「设置 → App Store → 沙盒账户」登录沙盒 Apple ID。点「创建下载链接」（或「我的 → 会员」）并订阅；沙盒的一年约 1 小时续期。到期后链接暂停，子域名恢复 7 天规则。新设备可用「恢复购买」；未订阅时点「创建下载链接」会弹出会员页。
5. 删除账号：「我的 → 删除账号」，立即删除名字、地址、链接及该设备在服务器上的全部数据。
6. HTML/Markdown 预览是本地文件预览，不是网页浏览器；默认禁止远程内容。
7. 无广告、无 ATT、无统计 SDK、无第三方追踪。后端为 https://stolnk.com，由开发者在 Cloudflare 上运营。
8. 加密：仅使用 Apple CryptoKit 的 P-256、AES-GCM、HKDF 做端到端加密，符合出口豁免。
